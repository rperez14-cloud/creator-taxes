Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RDPC Services LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit creatortaxpro.com (the "Site") or contact us about our tax services. We wrote it in plain language so it's easy to understand.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our consultation form, message us, or call us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Details about your income, platforms, and tax questions
  • Any other information you choose to share with us

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, device, pages visited, the website that referred you, and general location (city or country). This may be collected using cookies or similar tools, including analytics and advertising tools such as Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and TikTok.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and schedule your free consultation
  • To provide tax preparation, bookkeeping, and related services if you become a client
  • To send you information you asked for, such as reminders or helpful tax tips (you can opt out at any time)
  • To understand how visitors use the Site and improve it
  • To measure how well our ads and social media posts work
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations

3. Your tax information is confidential

As a tax preparer, we take the confidentiality of your tax information seriously. We follow federal rules that limit how tax preparers may use and share tax return information, including Internal Revenue Code Section 7216. We will not use or share your tax return information for any purpose other than preparing your return or providing the services you requested, unless you give us written consent or the law requires it.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it in these limited situations:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as our online form provider (Jotform), website host (GitHub Pages), email and messaging services (including WhatsApp), scheduling tools, secure document portals, and tax software. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Government agencies, such as the IRS or state tax agencies, when we file returns or respond to notices on your behalf.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights.

5. Cookies and your choices

Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some parts of the Site may not work as expected. You can also learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

6. How we protect your information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including secure systems for collecting and storing tax documents. No method of sending or storing information online is 100% secure, so please don't send sensitive documents, like Social Security numbers or tax forms, by regular email, text, or WhatsApp. We'll give you a secure way to share them.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as we need it to provide our services and to meet legal, tax, and professional record-keeping requirements. When we no longer need it, we delete it or store it securely.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • See the personal information we have about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information, unless we're required by law to keep it
  • Stop sending you marketing messages

To make a request, contact us using the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws.

9. Children's privacy

The Site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Links to other websites

The Site links to other websites and apps, such as Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Google, and Jotform. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top of this page.
